How to Incorporate It

Your Daily Routine

Use the Neck Pillow as part of the transition from daytime activity to bedtime — for example, while reading, listening to music, meditating, or practicing calm breathing.

Step 01

Position the center of the pillow behind the neck while seated.

Step 02

Keep the front of the throat open so breathing, swallowing, hearing, sight, and movement stay unrestricted.

Step 03

Begin with a short period and reposition or remove the pillow whenever desired.

Step 04

For a Recovery Sleep wind-down, remove the pillow before unattended or overnight sleep unless the current approved instructions expressly allow that use.

Step 05

Use during passenger travel only; never use while driving or operating machinery.

Directions for Use

Setting Up & Using Neck Pillow

The final product label and Instructions for Use control if they differ from this page.

  1. Inspect the fabric, seams, foam, closure, and cover before use.
  2. If directed, allow the foam to expand and air in a well-ventilated area after unpacking.
  3. Place the center behind the neck, with the sides resting comfortably beside the neck and away from the front of the throat.
  4. Do not force the head or neck into extension, flexion, rotation, or a fixed position.
  5. Use only during seated rest, reading, meditation, or passenger travel unless another position is expressly approved.
  6. Remove it right away if it feels uncomfortable, irritates the skin, or makes breathing or swallowing feel restricted.
  7. Clean only as directed on the care label; do not wash or immerse the foam core unless expressly permitted.

Safety Information & Limitations

Use Safely

  • For adult use only; keep away from infants and children because pillows and foam may create suffocation or entrapment hazards
  • Do not place over the mouth, nose, or front of the throat
  • Do not use while driving, cycling, walking, exercising, or operating machinery
  • Passenger use must not interfere with a seat belt, head restraint, safety equipment, or the ability to exit
  • Not designed to hold the head or neck in a fixed position
  • Do not use it if it interferes with breathing, swallowing, sight, hearing, movement, or any equipment you use
  • Keep away from open flame, heaters, high temperatures, and excessive moisture
