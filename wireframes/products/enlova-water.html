Directions for Use

Setting Up & Using ENLOVA™ Water

The final product label and Instructions for Use control if they differ from this page.

  1. Inspect the bottle and tamper-evident seal before opening.
  2. Do not consume if the seal is broken, the bottle leaks, or the water has an unusual appearance, odor, or taste.
  3. Open with clean hands and drink according to ordinary hydration needs.
  4. Refrigerate after opening and consume promptly. Do not share an opened bottle.
  5. Do not refill or repeatedly reuse a bottle designed for single use.
  6. Store unopened bottles in a clean, cool, dry area away from sunlight, chemicals, fuels, strong odors, pests, and excessive heat.
  7. Do not microwave, boil, or heat water in the original plastic bottle. Transfer it to a suitable heat-safe container if warming is desired.
  8. Follow the printed lot and best-by information and recycle the empty bottle and cap where accepted.

Safety Information & Limitations

Use Safely

  • Consume only from bottles with an intact tamper-evident seal.
  • Do not consume from a leaking, swollen, punctured, opened, damaged, or abnormal bottle.
  • Store away from sunlight, excessive heat, chemicals, gasoline, pesticides, cleaning products, and strong odors.
  • Do not leave cases in a hot vehicle for extended periods.
  • Do not microwave, boil, or heat water in the original plastic bottle.
  • Refrigerate after opening, consume promptly, and do not repeatedly refill a single-use bottle.
  • Keep caps and plastic packaging away from infants and young children.
  • Do not use for wound care, contact-lens care, nasal rinsing, injection, inhalation equipment, or another medical use.
  • Do not use as a substitute for prescribed oral rehydration products or medical treatment.
  • Retain the bottle, lot code, case, and purchase information when reporting contamination, illness, damage, or a quality concern.
