How to Incorporate It

Your Daily Routine

Use the Eyemask as a simple bedtime cue: dim the room, complete your usual wind-down activities, and place the clean, dry mask gently over closed eyes.

Step 01

Adjust the strap so the mask stays in place without pressing on the eyes, temples, ears, or nose.

Step 02

Begin with a short period to assess fit and skin comfort.

Step 03

Use during relaxation, meditation, quiet rest, ordinary sleep, or passenger travel in a safe environment.

Step 04

Remove it whenever you like, or if it feels uncomfortable, presses on the eyes, or irritates the skin.

Step 05

Keep the mask clean, dry, and for personal use only.

Directions for Use

Setting Up & Using Eyemask

The final product label and Instructions for Use control if they differ from this page.

  1. Inspect the fabric, seams, strap, closure, and labels before use.
  2. Confirm that the mask is clean and completely dry; remove makeup or creams that could soil the mask or irritate the skin.
  3. Close your eyes and position the mask gently without pressing on the eyeballs or obstructing the nose.
  4. Adjust the strap so it is snug but not tight, and so breathing, hearing, and removing the mask stay easy.
  5. Use only while resting, meditating, sleeping, or traveling as a passenger in a safe setting.
  6. Do not use while driving, walking, exercising, or operating machinery.
  7. Hand wash with mild soap and water as directed, lay flat, and allow the mask to air-dry completely before reuse.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, Answered

How does the Eyemask support a Recovery Sleep routine?

It helps create a darker, more consistent bedtime environment and a familiar cue for winding down. It does not guarantee a particular sleep result.

Does it guarantee better or deeper sleep?

No. No sleep duration, sleep stage, or outcome is promised.

Can it be worn overnight?

An adult may wear it during ordinary sleep if it remains comfortable, does not press on the eyes, does not interfere with breathing, and can be removed independently.

Can I use it while traveling?

Yes, while traveling as a passenger in a safe setting. Do not use it while driving, walking through a terminal, or whenever visual awareness is required.

Can it be heated or chilled?

No. Do not heat, microwave, refrigerate, or freeze it unless current instructions specifically permit that use.

How should it be cleaned?

Gently hand wash using mild soap and water, then lay it flat to air-dry completely.
