How to Incorporate It

Your Daily Routine

A parent or responsible adult may incorporate the product into bedtime, quiet reading, calm family time, or another ordinary wellness activity.

Step 01

A caregiver should place the device on a dry, level, stable surface near the child's sleeping area.

Step 02

Keep the unit outside the bed, away from bedding, and beyond the child's reach.

Step 03

Follow the placement distance and duration printed in the current instructions supplied with the product.

Step 04

Continue the family's usual wind-down routine; no touching or direct interaction is required.

Step 05

Inspect the unit and its placement periodically and move it only when the child is not handling it.

Directions for Use

Setting Up & Using Biophotonizer C

The final product label and Instructions for Use control if they differ from this page.

  1. An adult must unpack, inspect, position, clean, and move the product.
  2. Confirm that the housing and label are intact and that no damage is visible.
  3. Place the unit upright on a dry, stable surface where it cannot fall or be reached by the child.
  4. Never place it inside the child's bed, beneath a pillow, under bedding, or on an unstable or elevated ledge.
  5. No plug, battery, charging step, software, or wireless connection is required.
  6. Do not allow the child to hold, carry, throw, open, sit on, or play with the product.
  7. Discontinue use and contact Customer Support if the housing is cracked, leaking, open, deformed, or otherwise damaged.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, Answered

What age is Biophotonizer C intended for?

It is intended for children age five and older under adult supervision. The adult purchaser is responsible for placement, inspection, and use.

Is it a toy?

No. It is a parent-managed general-wellness product and should remain beyond the child's reach.

Does it require electricity or charging?

No. It is passive and does not require household power, batteries, charging, software, or wireless connectivity.

Does it need to touch the child?

No. Direct body contact is not required.

Can it be placed in the child's bed?

No. Keep it outside the bed, away from bedding, and on a stable surface beyond the child's reach.

Does it guarantee better sleep?

No. It may be included in a consistent bedtime environment, but no sleep duration, sleep stage, or result is promised.

Can multiple units be used?

Use only the quantity and configuration described in the current approved instructions. Do not assume that more units create greater coverage or better results.
