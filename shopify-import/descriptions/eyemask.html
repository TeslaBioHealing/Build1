Create darkness, comfort, and a familiar cue for winding down

Tesla BioHealing® Eyemask is a non-powered adult comfort accessory designed to reduce ordinary ambient light during relaxation, meditation, passenger travel, and a consistent Recovery Sleep routine.

It does not mean recovery from an illness, injury, or sleep disorder, and no particular sleep outcome is promised or guaranteed.

Your Daily Routine

  • Adjust the strap so the mask stays in place without pressing on the eyes, temples, ears, or nose.
  • Begin with a short period to assess fit and skin comfort.
  • Use during relaxation, meditation, quiet rest, ordinary sleep, or passenger travel in a safe environment.
  • Remove it whenever desired or if it causes pressure, warmth, irritation, blurred vision, headache, or discomfort.
  • Keep the mask clean, dry, and for personal use only.

Use the Eyemask as a simple bedtime cue: dim the room, complete your usual wind-down activities, and place the clean, dry mask gently over closed eyes.

Directions for Use

  1. Inspect the fabric, seams, strap, closure, and labels before use.
  2. Confirm that the mask is clean and completely dry; remove makeup or creams that could soil the mask or irritate the skin.
  3. Close your eyes and position the mask gently without pressing on the eyeballs or obstructing the nose.
  4. Adjust the strap so breathing, hearing, circulation, and independent removal remain unrestricted.
  5. Use only while resting, meditating, sleeping, or traveling as a passenger in a safe setting.
  6. Do not use while driving, walking, exercising, or operating machinery.
  7. Hand wash with mild soap and water as directed, lay flat, and allow the mask to air-dry completely before reuse.

Safety Information and Limitations

  • For adult use only; keep the mask, strap, and packaging away from infants and young children
  • The wearer must be able to remove the mask independently
  • Do not tighten excessively, press on the eyeballs, or obstruct the mouth or nose
  • Do not use while driving, walking, exercising, or operating machinery
  • The mask is not protection from sunlight, lasers, ultraviolet radiation, particles, chemicals, or workplace hazards
  • Do not heat, microwave, refrigerate, freeze, or add substances unless current instructions expressly permit it
  • Do not place over injured, inflamed, infected, or recently treated skin
  • Stop use for eye pressure, blurred vision, pain, redness, itching, numbness, headache, breathing difficulty, skin irritation, or unusual discomfort
  • Do not share; treat the eyemask as a personal-use hygiene product
