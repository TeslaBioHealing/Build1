Four-Unit Recovery Sleep™ Wellness-Space System

Tesla BioHealing® Cornerstone is an optional, passive, noninvasive four-unit LIPG system designed to help adults create a dedicated environment for Recovery Sleep™, consistent relaxation habits, and responsible self-care.

General-Wellness Features

Cornerstone provides a coordinated four-unit format for an adult bedroom, meditation room, living area, or designated wellness space.

  • Creates a clear environmental cue to begin an evening wind-down
  • Complements consistent bedtime and waking schedules
  • Accompanies reading, meditation, breathing, or quiet reflection
  • Supports a calm, uncluttered space for ordinary relaxation
  • Operates passively without household electricity, batteries, charging, or software
  • Allows adults to share a nonmedical general-wellness space
  • Fits alongside personal vitality, balance, or qi-related wellness practices
  • Uses four coordinated units positioned according to the approved placement guide

Passive: no lasers, no heat, no cords, nothing to set up. Individual experiences vary, and no particular result is promised.

Your Daily Routine

Prepare for Rest: Dim the lights, reduce unnecessary noise, and choose a calm activity such as reading, breathing, meditation, or reflection.

Protect the Night: Keep the room comfortable and uncluttered, maintain a consistent bedtime, and leave all four units safely positioned according to the approved diagram.

Begin Again: Rise at a consistent time, keep exits and walkways clear, and return to ordinary daytime activities.

Shared Wellness Time: Adults may use the space for quiet reflection, reading, meditation, gentle stretching, or other ordinary nonmedical activities.

Direct body contact and active interaction are not required.

Directions for Use

  1. Confirm that the shipment contains all four Cornerstone units and the required instructions.
  2. Inspect every unit, strap, handle, housing, and package for visible damage.
  3. Each unit weighs approximately 27 lb. Use appropriate lifting methods, moving equipment, and assistance.
  4. Do not lift, carry, drag, or support a unit by the decorative handle.
  5. Place each unit flat on a dry, level, stable floor capable of supporting its weight.
  6. Arrange all four units around the selected wellness area exactly as shown in the final approved placement diagram.
  7. Keep units away from beds, furniture, stairs, exits, walkways, heat, water, and unstable surfaces as directed.
  8. Do not sit, stand, sleep, stack, or place furniture on the units.
  9. No plug, battery, charging, software, wireless connection, or direct body contact is required.
