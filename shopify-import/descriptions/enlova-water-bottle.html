A Reusable Bottle for Mindful Everyday Hydration

ENLOVA™ Water Bottle is a reusable food-contact container designed to hold plain potable water and complement an adult's mindful everyday hydration routine.

General-Wellness Features

Designed for simple, intentional everyday use, the ENLOVA™ Water Bottle:

  • Holds plain potable water for everyday drinking
  • Encourages a consistent, mindful hydration routine
  • Provides a reusable alternative to repeatedly purchasing single-use bottles
  • Uses a compact, substantial format for a stable home or office surface
  • Requires no electricity, batteries, charging, software, or wireless connection
  • Can be cleaned and reused according to approved care instructions
  • May contain a sealed, non-food-contact LIPG-related component, subject to verification

No claim is made that the bottle changes water, improves absorption, increases hydration efficiency, adds nutrients, alters pH, transfers photons, removes contaminants, or produces a health benefit.

Who May Enjoy It

The bottle may appeal to adults who:

  • Want a reusable container for plain drinking water
  • Are developing a mindful hydration routine
  • Prefer a non-powered bottle
  • Want a substantial bottle for home, office, or another stable indoor setting
  • Understand that the bottle is not a filter or water-treatment system
  • Are willing to hand wash and inspect it regularly

It is not intended for infants, young children, infant formula, medication, medical nutrition, oral rehydration treatment, purification, disinfection, or unapproved beverages.

Your Daily Routine

Morning: Fill the clean bottle with fresh potable water within the approved temperature range.

During the Day: Keep it on a stable desk or countertop and drink according to personal preference.

Refill: Use fresh potable water as needed. No activation, charging, waiting period, or special arrangement is required.

End of Day: Empty unused water, hand wash the approved food-contact components, rinse thoroughly, and air-dry completely.

Individual water needs vary with diet, activity, environment, health status, and professional advice. No universal quantity is recommended.

Directions for Use

  1. Inspect the bottle, lid, gasket, food-contact chamber, seals, and packaging before first use.
  2. Hand wash washable food-contact components with mild dish soap and warm water; rinse and air-dry completely.
  3. Fill only with fresh potable water within the approved temperature range and do not exceed the fill line.
  4. Secure the lid without overtightening and keep the bottle upright unless leak testing supports another orientation.
  5. Use fresh water according to personal preference. No waiting or charging period is required or claimed.
  6. Empty unused water within the approved storage period and wash after each day of use.
  7. Do not use a dishwasher, microwave, oven, stove, freezer, boiling water, or another heat source unless expressly validated.
  8. Do not use hot, carbonated, fermented, alcoholic, acidic, dairy, juice, or perishable liquids unless separately approved.
  9. Discontinue use if the bottle develops damage, corrosion, leakage, unusual discoloration, odor, residue, or taste.

Safety Information and Limitations

  • For adult use with plain potable water only. This bottle does not make unsafe water safe.
  • Handle carefully; the empty bottle is heavy (approximately 2.2 lb, subject to confirmation).
  • Do not allow young children to carry or play with it.
  • Do not use for infant formula, medication, medical nutrition, or oral rehydration treatment.
  • Do not use hot or carbonated liquids unless temperature and pressure safety are validated.
  • Do not microwave, boil, bake, freeze, expose to flame, or place in a dishwasher unless expressly approved.
  • Do not cut, drill, puncture, crush, dismantle, or modify the bottle.
  • Any internal activated material must remain sealed and isolated from the water. Stop use if it becomes exposed.
  • Inspect the bottle, lid, gasket, interior, and seals regularly and discontinue use if damaged or abnormal.
  • Wash, rinse, and dry regularly and do not share without appropriate cleaning.
