Recovery Sleep™ Comfort Pillow for Adult Bedtime Routines

Tesla BioHealing® Pillow is an optional adult comfort product within Recovery Sleep™. It provides ordinary cushioning and may incorporate verified Tesla BioHealing® LIPG material as a passive general-wellness feature.

General-Wellness Features

The pillow is designed to make an adult bedtime setting comfortable and inviting.

  • Provides ordinary cushioning for the head and neck
  • Offers Soft and Firm choices for individual comfort preference
  • Uses a standard-size format suitable for many conventional pillowcases
  • Complements consistent bedtime and waking routines
  • Requires no electricity, batteries, charging, software, or setup
  • May be used for nighttime rest or quiet relaxation
  • May incorporate verified Tesla BioHealing® LIPG material as a passive wellness feature

No faster sleep onset, longer sleep, improved sleep stages, greater energy, improved cognition, better circulation, or accelerated recovery is promised.

Who May Enjoy It

The pillow may appeal to adults who:

  • Want a standard-size pillow for ordinary sleep comfort
  • Are creating a calmer and more consistent bedtime environment
  • Prefer a softer or firmer feel
  • Use reading, breathing, meditation, or quiet time before bed
  • Prefer a non-powered wellness accessory
  • Want a made-to-order pillow in a familiar residential format

Fit and comfort depend on anatomy, sleep position, mattress firmness, and personal preference. Anyone requiring medical positioning, airway management, postsurgical support, or treatment should consult a qualified professional.

Your Daily Routine

Prepare for Rest: Place the pillow in a clean, dry pillowcase, dim the lights, and allow time for reading, breathing, meditation, or quiet relaxation.

Protect the Night: Keep the room comfortable, quiet, and darkened; maintain a consistent bedtime; and use the selected firmness in a comfortable position.

Begin Again: Rise at a consistent time, air the pillow as appropriate, and return to ordinary daytime activities.

Care: Keep the pillow clean and dry and follow separate approved instructions for the cover and internal core.

The pillow is an optional comfort element. Healthy, repeatable habits remain the foundation of Recovery Sleep™.

Directions for Use

  1. Confirm that the selected Soft or Firm version was delivered and inspect the packaging, pillow, seams, closure, and labels.
  2. Remove the pillow carefully from its vacuum-sealed packaging and place it in a clean, dry, ventilated area.
  3. Allow up to 72 hours for expansion only if that period appears in the final approved instructions.
  4. Place the fully expanded pillow in a clean, appropriately sized pillowcase.
  5. Position it beneath the head and neck in a way that feels comfortable for the user's normal sleep position.
  6. Do not force the head or neck into a particular alignment or stack pillows to a height that interferes with breathing, swallowing, or movement.
  7. Stop use or reposition the pillow if it causes pain, numbness, tingling, breathing difficulty, skin irritation, headache, dizziness, or unusual discomfort.
  8. Follow the care label. Do not immerse the internal core, machine-wash it, or expose it to high heat unless expressly permitted.
