Your Daily Routine

Keep It Available: Store unopened bottles in a clean, cool, dry area away from sunlight, heat, chemicals, fuels, strong odors, pests, and contamination.

Inspect Before Opening: Confirm that the bottle, cap, and tamper-evident seal are intact.

Drink Normally: Consume according to personal preference, activity, environment, diet, and professional advice. No universal number of bottles is recommended.

After Opening: Refrigerate and consume promptly. Do not share or repeatedly refill a single-use bottle.

No charging, activation, waiting period, geometric arrangement, or special placement is required.

Directions for Use

  1. Inspect the bottle and tamper-evident seal before opening.
  2. Do not consume if the seal is broken, the bottle leaks, or the water has an unusual appearance, odor, or taste.
  3. Open with clean hands and drink according to ordinary hydration needs.
  4. Refrigerate after opening and consume promptly. Do not share an opened bottle.
  5. Do not refill or repeatedly reuse a bottle designed for single use.
  6. Store unopened bottles in a clean, cool, dry area away from sunlight, chemicals, fuels, strong odors, pests, and excessive heat.
  7. Do not microwave, boil, or heat water in the original plastic bottle. Transfer it to a suitable heat-safe container if warming is desired.
  8. Follow the printed lot and best-by information and recycle the empty bottle and cap where accepted.
