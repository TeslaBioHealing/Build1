Bringing anything new into your center is a business decision. You need to know it will be used, that it fits your brand, and that it won't create extra work or risk.

Most wellness products bury those answers under sales language. Guess wrong, and you're left with a box in the closet and an awkward conversation with your team.

So here are straight answers.

Will my team need training or setup time?

No. It's passive: no cords, nothing to switch on, and nothing to adjust. Unpack it, place it, and you're done.

Will it fit the look and feel of my center?

The solid-state units have a clean, minimal gold finish designed to sit quietly in session rooms, waiting areas, and relaxation rooms.

How many units does my center need?

That depends on your space, not on any health goal. On a call, we look at your rooms and layout and recommend a setup that fits.

What can my team say to clients?

We provide approved wellness wording, so your team can talk about it with confidence. It's a general wellness product designed to support relaxation, comfort, and everyday well-being. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

Can clients use it at home?

Yes. The QiRenew System is designed to create a relaxing, wellness-supportive environment at home, too, which gives your center a natural way to stay part of clients' everyday routine.

What happens on the call?

A focused, 30-minute conversation with Janno about your center, your rooms, and your goals. If it's not a fit, you'll know quickly, and that's a perfectly good outcome.
