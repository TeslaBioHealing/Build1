Practitioners looking at something new for their space tend to ask the same handful of practical questions.

The answers are usually buried in sales pages or vague marketing language, which makes it hard to tell whether something is even worth a conversation.

So here are straight answers to the questions we hear most often.

Does it need power or setup?

No. It's passive: no cords, nothing to switch on, and nothing to set up.

Does it produce heat, or anything I need to manage?

No lasers and no heat. The QiRenew System is solid-state and designed to create a calming biophoton environment, and it sits quietly in the background.

Is it meant to be part of a client's health plan?

No. It's a general wellness product designed to support relaxation, comfort, and everyday well-being. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it doesn't replace anything you or a client's doctor does.

How many would I need?

There's no “right amount” for any health goal, and we won't suggest one. If we talk, it's about your space: its size, its layout, and where clients rest.

Can it be used at home, too?

Yes. The QiRenew System is designed to support the flow of Qi (“energy”) and create a relaxing, wellness-supportive environment at home. Some people place it near their bed, chair, or workspace as part of their everyday routine.

What happens on a call?

A relaxed, 30-minute conversation with Janno about your space and any questions you still have. If it's not a fit, that's a perfectly good outcome too.

Whether or not QiRenew is right for your practice, we hope these answers are useful.
