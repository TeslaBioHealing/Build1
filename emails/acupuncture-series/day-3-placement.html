Practical placement

Where it fits in a working practice

Adding anything new to a working practice usually comes with strings attached: setup, cords, training, upkeep, or a change to how your sessions run.

When your schedule is already full, even small changes add friction. And anything that needs regular attention tends to end up pushed into a closet within a few months.

Designed to stay in the background

The QiRenew System is passive, so there's nothing for you or your clients to do. It's simple to incorporate into relaxation rooms and other wellness spaces, and it sits quietly where you place it.

Three common places

1
 The waiting area
Placed near seating to create a calm, relaxing environment from the moment clients arrive.
2
 The relaxation room
Positioned near a chair or quiet corner where clients unwind before heading back into their day.
3
 Your own workspace
Many practitioners start with one near their own desk or chair, so they can experience the environment for themselves first.
 

A few practical notes

There are no cords, no heat, and nothing to switch on, so it won't add steps to your day or change how you run your sessions.

We also don't recommend a number of units or hours for any health goal. Placement is simply about comfort and the feel of the room, and that's a conversation about your space, not about anyone's health.
