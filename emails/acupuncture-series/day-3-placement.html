Every acupuncture center owner knows the math: rooms turn over all day, and your team's time belongs to clients.

New equipment usually means setup, training, cords, upkeep, and a few extra minutes per session. Across a full week, that adds up, and anything that needs regular attention tends to end up pushed into a closet.

Passive by design

The QiRenew System is passive: no cords, no heat, nothing to switch on, and nothing for your staff to learn. Place it once and it simply stays part of the room.

Where centers place it

1
 Session rooms
Placed along the walls or near the table, out of the way of your work.
2
 The waiting area
Near seating, so a calm, relaxing environment greets clients at check-in.
3
 The relaxation room
Near a chair or quiet corner where clients unwind before heading back into their day.
 

We size each setup to your center: its rooms, its layout, and where clients spend their time. We don't recommend a number of units or hours for any health goal. It's about the feel of your space.
