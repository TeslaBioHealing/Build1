Your relationship with a client is strongest during their visit. Then they go home, and for days or weeks, your center isn't part of their day at all.

That's when routines drift and other options start to look appealing. Clients ask what they can do at home, and if you don't have an answer, they find one somewhere else, usually from a store or website that has nothing to do with your center.

A natural recommendation from your center

The QiRenew System is designed to support the flow of Qi (“energy”) and create a relaxing, wellness-supportive environment at home. Clients can place it near their bed, chair, or workspace as part of their everyday routine, and every time they see it, they're reminded of your center.

Talking about it the right way

We give partner centers clear, approved wording so every recommendation stays focused on wellness: relaxation, comfort, calm, everyday well-being, and the flow of Qi. If a client asks about a health concern, the answer is simple:

Example wording
“Thanks for asking. It isn't designed for any medical condition, so I can't say that, and your doctor is the best person to ask. Many people simply enjoy it as part of a calm, relaxing space at home.”

Clear wording protects your center's reputation and keeps every conversation honest, which is exactly what clients expect from you.
