How to talk about it with your clients

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Clients notice anything new in your space, and they ask about it, often with a personal health question attached.

Without clear wording ready, a well-meaning answer can easily drift into promises about health or results that no wellness product should make. That puts you in an awkward spot, and it isn't fair to the client either.

The words we use

We've put a lot of care into how we describe QiRenew, and we're happy to share that language with you: relaxation, comfort, calm, everyday well-being, a restful sleep routine, and the flow of Qi (“energy”). We stay away from anything about health conditions, symptoms, or medical results, and we don't compare it to medical devices.

If a client asks about a health concern

1
 Acknowledge
Thank them for asking. There's no need to comment on their concern.
2
 Clarify
Explain that it's a general wellness device, not designed for any medical condition, and that their doctor is the best person for health questions.
3
 Refocus
Bring it back to comfort, relaxation, and the calm of the space.
 
Example wording
“Thanks for asking. It isn't designed for any medical condition, so I can't say that, and your doctor is the best person to ask. Many people simply enjoy it as part of a calm, relaxing space.”

The one-question test

Before describing it, we ask ourselves one question: would this sentence still make sense to someone with no health concerns at all? If it would, it's wellness language. If it wouldn't, we rephrase it.
